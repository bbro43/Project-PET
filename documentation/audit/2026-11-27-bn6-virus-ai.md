# BN6 virus AI audit (from 2026-11-27)

The virus AI table is at 0x8109050: one pointer per AI byte × 4 (1 Mettaur … 23 Gunner … 29 Nightmare). Each family's table follows the Navi layout: 0 spawn, 1–7 shared states, 8 decision, 9 wait (generic 0x81097BA), 0xA+ attacks.
Per-version tables have 6 entries in variant order: base, 2, 3, SP, Rare, Rare2.
Attack damage comes from each AI's own table. The stat records' third field is not the attack damage.
