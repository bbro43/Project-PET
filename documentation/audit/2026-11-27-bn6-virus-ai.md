# BN6 virus AI audit (from 2026-11-27)

The virus AI table is at 0x8109050: one pointer per AI byte × 4 (1 Mettaur … 23 Gunner … 29 Nightmare). Each family's table follows the Navi layout: 0 spawn, 1–7 shared states, 8 decision, 9 wait (generic 0x81097BA), 0xA+ attacks.
Per-version tables have 6 entries in variant order: base, 2, 3, SP, Rare, Rare2.
Attack damage comes from each AI's own table. The stat records' third field is not the attack damage.

## Mettaur (AI 1, 0x8109EF4)
- Decision 0x8109FD6, states:
  - 0x810A004: the turn token at ExtraVars+4 (0x800AA06). Without the token: version > 0 → 0xC (hide). On the first turn: action 9 for 0x1E frames. Then MegaMan's row is compared.
  - 0x810A080: 0xA row step, with wait 0x8109F46 (30/24/18/12/18/12).
  - 0x810A126: 0xB, with Unk_0c = version (0x8109F40) and damage 0x8109F28 (10/30/50/70/50/100).
- 0xA (0x8109CE6): vanish effect, 3 frames, move, 3 frames, then wait.
- 0xB (0x8109DD2): animation 1 for 0x40 frames; the counter window at 0x32; shockwave type3 0x16 spawned at 0x1B on the panel ahead. Then 0x28 frames, then the token is released.
- 0xC (0x8109E7A): animation 2 for 0x22, then animation 3 with the guard flag until the token is free, then animation 4 for 0x35.
- Shockwave type3 0x16 (0x80C6B40), table 0x80C6B00 by version: sprite 0x10/3, animation 0/1/1/2/1/2, frames per panel 22/16/11/6/16/6.

## Champy (AI 8, 0x810CD60)
- Decision 0x810CE1A:
  - After an attack (ExtraVars set): action 9 with 0x810CDA8 (30/18/18/12/18/6).
  - Otherwise 0x810971A finds MegaMan in its row; 0x810CEAE needs the panel in front of him free. Then 0xB with: Unk_18 0x810CD9C (42/24/12/6/6/6), Unk_0c 0x810CD96 (21/18/12/6/6/6), Unk_0d 0x810CDA2 (18/6/6/6/6/6), damage 0x810CDAE (20/30/50/80/30/90).
- 0xB (0x810C9B8):
  - Vanish and wait Unk_0d − 10. Reserve the panel in front of MegaMan, 0x0D frames, appear.
  - Wait, then punch (animation 2; hit via 0x810CD38 on frame 3).
  - Version > 1: a second spot (callback Unk_30) and animation 4 with the hit on frame 5.
  - Hold Unk_18, return home, hold Unk_18.
- Estimated: the role of Unk_0c (read as the pause before the punch) and the punch animation length (15 frames).

  - State 4 (0x8113162): only if MegaMan is in its row → 0xA.
  - State 8 (0x81131A2) → 0xB.
- Parameters by version: shots 0x81130D8 (3/5/3/7/3/7), lock 0x81130C6 (24/24/12/12/12/12), recover 0x81130D2 (24/24/12/12/24/6), damage 0x81130C0 (10/30/50/70/40/90), crosshair speed 0x81130A8 (3/1.31/4/2.31/4.31/3 px per frame).
- 0xA (0x8112F4E): animation 1. The crosshair (type4 0x30) starts on Gunner's own panel and slides along the row. When it reaches MegaMan it reports, and animation 2 starts, then one shot every 0x0A frames (0x80DFF84), then animation 3 for the recover time.
- 0xB (0x8112D9C): the crosshair starts on the first panel of the opponent area (0x8112E24 filter). After each shot it steps one panel (0x8112EFA) and reverses at the edge.
- Estimated: the crosshair's lock time (Timer2 = Unk_0c, read as the delay before firing).
