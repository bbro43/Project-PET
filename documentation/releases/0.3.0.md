# 0.3.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 3)

- Virus Battle now lets you pick virus, variant (all six, with in-game names) and count 1–3.
- New viruses: Champy family (warps in front of MegaMan and punches) and Gunner family (crosshair tracks, locks on, fires a 3-shot burst).
- All virus HP, element and attack damage now read from the BN6 ROM tables for 29 families × 6 variants. Fixes Mettaur2/Mettaur3 damage (was 30/50, ROM says 20/40).
- Variant colours use the game's own palette slots.
- Mettaur ShockWave and Gunner crosshair now use the original effect sprites when a ROM is imported.
- Mettaur pause between actions uses the per-variant table from the Mettaur AI code.
- Element weakness (aqua > fire > wood > elec > aqua) doubles damage.
- Survival mixes Mettaur, Champy and Gunner and climbs to Ω variants.
- Asset pack 3: new data/bn6-viruses.mjs (generated by tools/extract_bn6_viruses.py), registry entries for Champy, Gunner, ShockWave and the crosshair.
- Tests: 22 engine tests + 2 local ROM tests.
