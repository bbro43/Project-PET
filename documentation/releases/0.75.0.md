# 0.75.0 — More values from the game, and batch 15

## Fixed (now read from the game)
- **Time freeze timing:** the screen now dims for 16 frames and the chip name stays up for 59 frames: it slides in for 5, holds 48 and slides out for 5. Both were estimates before (the name was 40). Every special chip's freeze is about 20 frames longer as a result.
- **Paralysis lengths:** these now come from the game's status table (status 0x10 = 90 frames, 0x11 = 120, 0x12 = 150). The app used to guess 120 for all of them.
  - 90 frames: WhiCapsl, Thunder, ElcPuls1, DarkMech's elec ball, FlshBom1 and Sensor's laser.
  - 150 frames: FlshBom2, FlshBom3 and DestPuls. DestPuls didn't paralyze at all before.
- **Sensor's laser:**
  - Each of the three aims now has its own animation and height, taken from the game's tables.
  - Each laser panel stays out until it hits something once, or until the sensor leaves.
  - The laser paralyzes the enemy for 90 frames.
- **ElcPuls2:** the game's data confirms it pulls the enemy exactly 1 panel toward MegaMan.

## New chips
- **Snake:** inside the freeze, each hole on MegaMan's side sends a snake, starting from the back column. Each snake rises, waits, bites, then flies at the closest enemy by row, then by column, and hits for 30. The next snake follows 8 frames later in the same column, 24 otherwise. With no holes nothing comes out.
- **GrabBnsh / GrabRvng:** every one of MegaMan's panels the enemy has stolen goes back. Then every enemy is struck once per panel returned, 30 frames apart. The CPU enemies here never steal panels, so in practice these only stop time.
- **Anubis:** a statue (100 HP) drops onto the panel ahead and stays for 50 seconds. It hits every enemy in the enemy area for 1 damage every 6 frames.
