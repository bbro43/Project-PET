New CPU Navi: BlastMan (BN6) — BlastMan, EX, SP, RV, BX (400 / 800 / 1400 / 2000 HP, Fire element). His AI is decoded from table entry 0x80FD104.
- Moves 3 times between attacks.
- Fire punch: steps to the front of MegaMan's row and punches a blast onto the panel in front of him (10 / 50 / 90 / 140).
- Fireball: lights 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 panels ahead, bursts into flame, then kicks a fireball across them (10 / 50 / 90 / 140). If one of his blasts is sitting right in front of MegaMan, he goes straight to this.
- Fire storm: from his back column he charges for 2 seconds, then fire sweeps the whole field from one edge (20 / 80 / 150 / 270). The base version always sweeps the rows from the right. EX and up can also sweep from the left, or down the columns from the top or bottom. The ROM's layouts leave no gap, so a shield or invincibility is the way through.
- His blasts and fireballs use sequences from his own sprite. His sprite is LZ77-compressed in the ROM and decodes on the device.
