# 0.59.0 — Chip animations, batch 2: bombs, seeds, panel shots, SuprVulc

## Bombs
- Thrown bombs fly 40 frames (the game's timer, was a guessed 30) and land 3 panels ahead. One that lands in a hole fizzles.
- **BigBomb:** 3×3 explosion.
- **EnergBom / MegEnBom:** the landing panel is hit 3 times, 7 frames apart.

## Seeds
- PoisSeed, IceSeed and GrasSeed turn the 3×3 area they land on into poison, ice or grass.
- If a seed hits someone, only that panel changes.

## Panel shots
- **DublShot:** lifts the panel in front and the one after it, and sends both down the row.
- **TrplShot:** lifts the whole front column.

## Vulcan
- SuprVulc fires 10 shots.
