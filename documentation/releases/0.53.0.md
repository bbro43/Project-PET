# 0.53.0 — Navi Customizer

New **Navi Customizer** button on the main menu.

## The screen (the game's own, decoded from the imported BN6 ROM)
- The NAVICUSTOMIZER frame, the colour bar, the 5×5 board with its frame and command line, and the INFORMATION box. Gregar's screen is green, Falzar's orange (PET theme 0).
- Parts are drawn the way the game does it: each board cell's tiles have their own palette entries, which take the part's colour. Plus Parts show their pattern colour, and frame cells go grey when a part covers them.
- The cursor and the Move / Remove menu are the game's sprites. The parts list and the INFORMATION text use the battle font; the INFORMATION wording is the app's own.

## Parts (rom-verified, data/bn6-navicust.mjs)
- All 46 programs in every colour the game has (names from the ROM), and an unlimited supply of each, like the Pack.
- **Cross parts** (owner decision): one per BN6 Cross (Heat, Elec, Slash, Erase, Charge, Spout, Tomahawk, Tengu, Ground, Dust). App-made 1×1 parts, each colour-coded. A Cross part works like a program (it has to touch the command line), it is exempt from the colour rules, and only one counts. It records the chosen Cross; fighting as that Cross comes with the next roadmap step.
- Controls:
  - In the list: A takes a part; Select toggles compression (the app lets every part compress); ← moves to the board.
  - Holding a part: the D-pad moves it, L/R turns it, A places it, B puts it back.
  - On the board: A on a part gives Move / Remove.
  - Start: RUN, which shows the bugs and saves.
  - Taps work too.

## Rules (the game's, see documentation/audit/2026-11-27-bn6-navicust.md)
- **Board:** 5×5 (both Expand Memory upgrades) inside a frame. Parts may sit on the frame, but that bugs.
- **Command line:** Programs must touch it; Plus Parts must stay off it.
- **Colours:** parts of the same colour may not touch, and using 5 or more colours bugs.
- **Groups:** Shield / Reflect / AntiDmg and the body programs are exclusive groups.
- **BugStop** removes all bugs.
- Bug types are counted and shown on RUN with their levels. Only three have a name in the disassembly (Processing, Emotion, Custom); the others show as "Bug type N". What bugs do in battle isn't in yet.

## Effects in battle
- **Applied now:**
  - HP+ (max HP);
  - Attack (buster Attack + 1, charge shot (Attack + 1) × 10);
  - Speed (the buster's recovery from the game's Speed table, by distance to the target);
  - Charge (100 / 90 / 80 / 70 / 60 frames);
  - Custom (chips in the custom window, 5–8);
  - MegFldr / GigFldr (the folder's MegaChip / GigaChip limits in Folder Edit);
  - SuperArmor (no flinch);
  - UnderShirt (a hit from above 1 HP leaves 1 HP);
  - BustPack, BodyPack, FldrPak1/2.
- **Recorded but not yet applied:** FstBarr, Shield / Reflect / AntiDmg (B+Left), FlotShoe, AirShoes, the body programs, ChpShufl, NumbrOpn, Rush / Beat / Tango, the bug effects. Overworld-only programs (SneakRun, SlipRunr, Collect, Millions, Humor, Poem, AutoHeal) have nothing to do here.
- The Regular memory stays 4 MB: in BN6 it is raised by RegUP items, not Customizer parts.
