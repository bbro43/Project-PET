# 0.34.0 — panel types

BN6 panel types, decoded from the game (assets 37):

- **Cracked → broken**: a cracked panel breaks when you step off it; a hole can't be stood on and repairs itself after 10 seconds, blinking in its last second.
- **Poison**: 1 HP every 7 frames while standing on it (enemies too).
- **Holy**: damage halved.
- **Grass**: wood viruses heal on it; fire hits do double damage and burn it away.
- **Ice**: you slide until you leave the ice; an aqua hit while on ice freezes you for 80 frames.
- **Lava**: erupts on a 140-frame cycle (your side and the enemy side take turns): 40 frames of bubbling, then a 50-damage flame column (ROM sprite). Aqua cools it.
- New **Field** option in the battle menu: Normal, Cracked, Grass, Ice, Holy, Poison, Lava, Mixed (presets, not canonical stage layouts).
- **Cragger**'s punch now cracks the three panels it hits.
- **ElementMan**: his element names were wrong (the attacks themselves were right). The meteors are fire, the sparkles are aqua and now leave ice, the towers are wood and now leave grass. His colour cycle is none → aqua → wood → elec → fire.

Not yet: road (conveyor) panels, and the ROM's own panel graphics (panels are still drawn). The ice slide speed is estimated.

Tests: 172 passing (new tests/panels.test.mjs). Browser check of every field preset and a lava eruption: no errors.
