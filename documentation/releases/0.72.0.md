# 0.72.0 — ElemTrap's last gap, and batch 12

## Fixed
- **ElemTrap:** every elemental enemy attack now springs it as soon as it can hurt a panel, including the ones that only check MegaMan's own panel (HeatMan's pillars, ElementMan's strikes, shells, waves and the rest).

## New chips
- **Wind / Fan:** a box on the panel ahead (40 HP, 24 seconds). Every 12 frames a gust runs down one row, in the order middle, top, bottom. Wind pushes the first enemy in that row one panel back; Fan pulls it one panel forward, staying on the enemy side.
- **BurnSqr1–3:** a 2 × 2 cursor sweeps the field on its own, one panel every 6 frames: along the lower two rows from the back of the enemy area to MegaMan's edge, then back along the upper two rows. Press the chip button to burn those four panels (fire); after 5 seconds it fires by itself.
- **Magnum:** a cursor runs along the middle row from the front enemy column to the back, then down the second-to-last column. Press the chip button: while it runs along the row, the whole column is hit; while it runs down the column, the three panels of that row. The panels break (cracked where someone stands) and the screen shakes. After 5 seconds it fires by itself.

## Notes
- What each Wind / Fan gust object does in the game (t3 0x49) isn't decoded yet; the one-panel push is labelled as recalled.
