# 0.31.0 — last BN6 virus batch

All 29 BN6 virus families are now playable (assets 35).

- **FighterPlane**: flies across its area twice, then one plane at a time bombs all 9 panels of MegaMan's area in a snake (front column top to bottom, middle column bottom to top, back column top to bottom). ROM-verified path and damage; bomb gaps from the AI table.
- **DarkMech**: moves toward MegaMan's row, throws its sword down the row every third move (10 damage), heavy slash when MegaMan stands in front (reach estimated).
- **SnakeArm**: the fist snakes out one panel at a time toward MegaMan and grabs him. Fist uses the ROM fist sprite.
- **Armadillo**: slides to MegaMan's row, curls up and rolls back and forth along it 3–5 times; damage from the stats record.
- **Cragger**: lifts a boulder and lobs it onto MegaMan's panel; versions above the base move between throws. Flight time estimated.
- **Nightmare**: hidden for the first second, then teleports around and swings its scythe over its whole front column after a 30-frame warning.

Tests: 156 passing (per-family "attacks MegaMan within 30 seconds" across all versions, plus the FighterPlane bombing path).
