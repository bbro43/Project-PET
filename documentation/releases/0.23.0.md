# 0.23.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 26)

New CPU boss: Cybeast Falzar (BN6), AI entry 21 (0x810547C). HP comes from his spawn hook: 2000 / 2800 / 3200 / 4000. With this, all four bosses past the Navi block are playable: Colonel, Bass, Gregar and Falzar.
- Like Gregar, he holds the whole enemy area and can be hit on any of its panels.
- His pattern:
  - Two moves (he fades out and back in) before each attack.
  - Feather Shot or Gust, chosen at random: a whirlwind runs down your row (80 / 240), or your row is lit and then hit by a wing gust (100 / 300).
  - Every third round is Feather Rain: feathers fall on lit panels, the first on you; 8 / 16 / 20 / 30 of them by version (80 / 240).
  - After every third Feather Rain round comes his big attack. Above half HP that's Feather Storm: strikes that follow you (12–30 by version, 100 / 300). Below half HP, Storm and Drill Dive take turns; Drill Dive turns him into a drill and dives through your row (150 / 450).

Decoded: the decision counters, the HP override, every damage table and the timing tables.
Estimated: the attack shapes, which timing row is the warning, count or gap, and the hurtbox.

Sprite: four layers (back wing, body, head, front wing) plus his shadow; his drill form is its own animation. Only the Falzar ROM has his full sprite. With both BN6 versions imported, each Cybeast's sprite is borrowed from its own version's ROM, whichever ROM you pick for sprites.

Asset pack 26: the registry adds the Falzar sprite; the rules add his AI parameters.
Tests: 118 engine/Navi tests + 3 local ROM tests.
