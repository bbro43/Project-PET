# 0.16.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 17)

New CPU Navi: DiveMan (BN6) — DiveMan, EX, SP, RV, BX (500 / 1000 / 1500 / 1900 HP, Aqua element). His AI is decoded from table entry 0x80FDDB0.
- Swims submerged with only his periscope showing, never going straight back to the panel he just left, and with no pause between swims.
- Surfaces to attack: torpedoes (6 in 16) or just a short surfaced pause (10 in 16), three times in a row.
- Torpedoes: two torpedoes, each flying at the panel MegaMan was on (20 / 40 / 100 / 180).
- Big wave: every 4th surfacing he leaps with all of MegaMan's area lit, slams down, and a wave rolls down all three rows (30 / 60 / 150 / 270). As in the ROM, there is no safe row.
- His wave, torpedo and periscope come from his own sprite (LZ77-compressed, decoded on the device).

Not simulated yet: the object he places at the start of battle (10 / 20 / 50 / 90 damage; behaviour not decoded), and whether he can be hit while submerged.
Estimated: torpedo flight time and wave speed.
ROM quirk kept as-is: the pause after the torpedoes is read from code bytes in the original game (19 / 224 / 0 / 240 frames by version).

Asset pack 17: registry adds the DiveMan sprite and his AI parameters.
Tests: 88 engine/Navi tests + 3 local ROM tests.
