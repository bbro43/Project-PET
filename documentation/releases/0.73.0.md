# 0.73.0 — Wind / Fan from the game, Meteors and Mine

## Fixed
- **Wind / Fan gusts** now follow the game. Each gust is a visible object that flies down its row at 10 pixels a frame and stays on the enemy side.
  - Wind starts at the first enemy panel. Every enemy it passes slides back as far as it can.
  - Fan starts at the back edge. Every enemy it passes slides forward as far as the enemy side allows.
  - The gust uses the game's own sprite.

## New chips
- **Meteors:** inside the time freeze, 30 meteors fall one every 10 frames onto the enemy panels. They go column by column from the back edge and cycle through the panels. Each meteor sits high up for half a second, then drops in from behind and hits its panel with a small shake.
- **Mine:** inside the freeze, the mine hops between the free enemy panels every 2 frames for 2 seconds, then hides on the last one. It goes off when an enemy steps on that panel (200 damage), or when the panel breaks.
