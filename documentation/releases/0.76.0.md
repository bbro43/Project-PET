# 0.76.0 — Guardian

## New chips
- **Guardian:** a statue (1 HP) on the panel ahead, lasting 100 seconds.
  - When an attack hits it, time stops, its name shows, and it strikes the side opposite whoever hit it for 200. Then it crumbles.
  - Hit by MegaMan (his buster, a chip), it strikes every enemy.
  - Hit by an enemy attack aimed at its panel, it strikes MegaMan.
