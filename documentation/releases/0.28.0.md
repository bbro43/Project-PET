# 0.28.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 32)

Virus batch 3: five more families decoded from the ROM, all six versions each. They're in Virus Battle and in the Endless Survival rotation.

- **Catack** (AI 7): waits 120 → 60 frames, then rolls to another panel in its row and fires a cannon shell down the row. Only one Catack fires at a time. Damage 70 / 100 / 150 / 200 (Rare 130 / 200).
- **WindBox** (AI 9): doesn't attack. Its wind sends a gust down one row every 12 frames, cycling bottom → middle → top. WindBox (even versions) blows you toward your back column; VacuumFan (odd versions) pulls you toward the front.
- **Trumpy** (AI 10): hops 3 times, then plays for 120 frames (240 for the Rare2 version). What the song does depends on the version:
  - The base and Rare2 versions make the other viruses invincible while it plays.
  - The Rare version fully heals them near the end.
  - The other three apply status effects that aren't decoded yet, so they do nothing for now.
- **OldStove** (AI 11): slides slowly to your row and toward the front of its area, opens up for 38 frames, then breathes fire on the panel ahead for 90 → 180 frames. Damage 20 / 40 / 80 / 100 (Rare 60 / 100).
- **HauntedCandle** (AI 12): one candle at a time. Every 120 frames it marks your panel, which flashes for 36 → 24 frames, then a flame strikes it. Damage 50 / 80 / 130 / 150 (Rare 100 / 150).
