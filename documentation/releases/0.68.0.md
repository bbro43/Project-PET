# 0.68.0 — Chip animations, batch 9: placed bombs and the Dragons

- **Placed bombs:** BlkBomb, FlshBom1–3, LilBolr1–3 and BugBomb are thrown 3 panels ahead. Each becomes an object on that panel if the panel is solid and empty; otherwise it puffs out. The object blocks shots like an obstacle.
  - **BlkBomb:** sits there (100 HP) until a Fire attack hits it, then explodes on every enemy with its own damage as Fire. If nothing sets it off, it disappears after 100 seconds.
  - **FlshBom1–3:** after 1 second it flashes and paralyses every enemy, dealing its damage.
  - **LilBolr1–3:** a boiler that takes hits. After 3 seconds, or its third hit, it bursts over the 8 panels around it, dealing its damage plus whatever it absorbed.
  - **BugBomb:** after 1 second it bursts over the 8 panels around it. In the game it gives Navis a bug, but the CPU Navis here don't have bugs, so it does no harm.
- **HeatDrgn / ElecDrgn / AquaDrgn / WoodDrgn:** a dragon comes down the nearest enemy column from above the field, swings one column back, and goes up and away. Its head hits each panel it enters. It uses the game's dragon sprite in each element's colours, with four body pieces trailing.
