# 0.86.0 — SandWrm, GolmHit

- **SandWrm1–3** (sandwormAttack_80DEDE0): for each of the first four enemies, if the panel behind it is standable and empty, a worm (the SnakeArm virus's own object and sprite, palette by chip level) leaps out of that panel onto the enemy over 16 frames and hits it. An enemy in the back column, or with something behind it, is not attacked.
- **GolmHit1–3** (sub_80DB4B4, t3 0xAE): a hand (sprite 0x0C/0x62) drops on the middle panel of the enemies' second column.
  - If something is on that panel, it takes the chip's damage, the 8 panels around it take 100, the 3×3 cracks, and the screen shakes 45 frames.
  - If the panel is empty, nothing happens.
- Effects now last their own length when it is longer than 24 frames. ElecMan's aura now lasts its full 30 frames (ROM 0x1E).
