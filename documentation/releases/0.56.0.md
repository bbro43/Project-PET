# 0.56.0 — Emotions, the emotion window, Confused, Blind and Bubbled

## Emotion window
- The game's emotion window sits under the HP box: 32×16 face + 16×16 piece at (0, 18). It's decoded on device from the ROM, with its own palette per face.

## Emotions (base MegaMan; see the audit)
- **Full Synchro:** hit an enemy with a chip while it's starting an attack (a counter, 16-frame window). The next chip that can be boosted does double damage, then MegaMan is Normal again. Getting hit also ends it.
- **Angry:** after a hit of 300 or more, or 120 frames stuck under paralysis or a bubble. It lasts 600 frames; the next boostable chip does double damage and ends it. MegaMan is tinted red.
- **Tired:** no Full Synchro or Angry while it lasts. Only the emotion bug causes it here.
- Which chips can be boosted comes from each chip's ROM flags.

## Emotion bug
- Every 60 frames MegaMan's emotion changes, with the game's odds (the N1GP wiki's table, read from byte_8013E44).

## Colour bug, fixed
- The fourth status was wrong in 0.55.0. It's **Confused** (D-pad reversed), not Bubbled: the timer the game sets is the confusion timer.
- **Blind** now also hides the enemies' shots and obstacles.

## Bubbled
- MegaMan can't act for 150 frames or until a hit lands. Elec does double damage, and mashing gets him out sooner.
- StarFish's bubbles cause it. That link isn't confirmed in the ROM yet.

## Not exact yet
- The counter ring and the Full Synchro flash are app-drawn.
- Which enemy states open the counter window is the app's reading.
- Beast Out isn't in the app, so the emotion bug always runs.
