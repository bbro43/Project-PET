# 0.57.0 — RegUP parts, the buster bug as the game does it, AntiDmg's shuriken

## RegUP (owner decision)
- New app-made Navi Customizer parts RegUP1, RegUP2 and RegUP3. They're 1×1 white Plus Parts, so they can go anywhere on the board.
- Each raises Regular memory by 1, 2 or 3 MB, using the game's formula: 4 + RegUP1 + 2×RegUP2 + 3×RegUP3, at most 99 (sub_803CFB0).
- The Folder screens show the new Regular memory.
- A Regular chip that no longer fits isn't put in the first hand.

## Buster bug, corrected
- The game rolls on every normal buster shot, not on the charge shot.
- With the bug, a shot may misfire (buster pose, no shot; family 0x33) or come out as a full charge shot (family 0x16). The odds are the game's 6, 10 or 13 in 16 misfires by level.

## AntiDmg
- The thrown star is now the game's shuriken (t3 0xC2, sprite 0x0C/0x2F). It falls onto the enemy and lands. In the game the program makes a hit count as the AntiDmg chip.

## Still open
- Reflect's and the bubble's missing colours. Those parts of the sprites use the next palette slot, which the game fills from somewhere not yet traced. They stay transparent.
