# 0.79.0 — BlzrdBal and LifeSync

## New chips
- **BlzrdBal:** a copy of MegaMan sets a snowball rolling down his row (aqua, 150). Once time runs again it rolls 3 pixels a frame and hits every enemy it passes. Any field object in its path (a cube, a statue, a box) gets swallowed, and each one adds another 150 and makes the ball bigger. It stops at a hole or the edge.
- **LifeSync:** a cursor lands 2 panels ahead of MegaMan. If an enemy stands there, the others are linked to it and their HP becomes its HP. With no enemy there, it does nothing. Cybeasts are left out.
