# 0.5.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 5)

ElecMan rebuilt from his decoded AI code:
- Coils: every 4th decision he blows up his coil and places a new one in MegaMan's area (15/20/40 HP — shoot them). When he fires along his row, any coil in that row discharges down its whole column.
- Row lightning: charge sparkle for 30 frames, then lightning across his row for 72 frames.
- Panel storm: groups of panels in MegaMan's area light up, then lightning strikes them in the same order (3 rounds, 4 below half HP).
- Dash strike: lines up with MegaMan's row, charges, dashes in at 10–13 px/frame and strikes the panel in front of him, then warps back.
- Glides between panels instead of teleporting.
- Removed the made-up target marker from 0.4.x; the game has none.

ProtoMan:
- Below half HP: standing on the middle-row panels in front of him builds toward his multi-slash; otherwise WideSword and LongSword alternate.
- EX and above step into MegaMan's row to guard.
- Both-swords attack is swung from his front column.

Visuals:
- Game sprites for ElecMan's coils, charge sparkle and lightning, and ProtoMan's sword slashes.
- Fix: attacks no longer stay frozen on screen after a battle ends.

Asset pack 5: registry adds coil, sparkle and sword-slash sprites; AI parameters updated.
Tests: 35 engine/Navi tests + 3 local ROM tests.
