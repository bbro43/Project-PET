# 0.29.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 33)

Virus batch 4: five more families decoded from the ROM, all six versions each, in Virus Battle and Endless Survival.

- **Kettle** (AI 13): the base Kettle never attacks. The stronger versions hop around (60 → 30 frames), and every third turn, if no other Kettle is busy, they appear next to you, boil, and burst steam over their panel and the eight around it. Damage 100 / 150 / 200 / 250 (Rare 100 / 150).
- **Puffy** (AI 14): drifts to a new panel every 20 frames, and every fourth time spits a bubble down its row. Damage 10 / 30 / 80 / 100 (Rare 50 / 100).
- **StarFish** (AI 15): when none of its bubbles are out, it releases three, 24 frames apart, that drift slowly down its row (1.3 → 1.6 px per frame). Then it waits 120 frames. Damage 20 / 40 / 100 / 120 (Rare 60 / 120).
- **Dragon** (AI 16): one at a time. It moves twice, then rises (40 frames), dives under (60 / 30 frames), and flies through your column from the top or bottom edge. The Rare versions make two passes. Damage 80 / 80 / 50 / 50 (Rare 100 / 200).
- **ScareCrow** (AI 17): every 180 frames (its panel flashes for the last 60) it heals itself to full and attacks; hitting it also sets it off. The attack winds up for 60 frames, then a doll-thunder runs down its row for 120. Damage 30 / 60 / 80 / 100 (Rare 70 / 110).

Fixed: a row projectile without its own sprite drew the whole virus. In 0.28.0, Catack's shells looked like tiny Catacks. Projectiles without a ROM sprite now use a simple shape.
