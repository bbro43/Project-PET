# 0.77.0 — Batch 16: BugFix, Otenko, CircGun

## New chips
- **BugFix:** clears MegaMan's NaviCust bugs, including the HP drain, for the rest of the battle. A copy of him flashes white, faster and faster, for 2 seconds, then half a second more, inside the time freeze.
- **Otenko:** Otenko (100 HP) appears on the panel ahead for 30 seconds. Every 5 frames he adds 1 to the damage of MegaMan's next chip, up to +50 for the same chip. Only chips that can take a bonus get it.
- **CircGun:** inside the freeze, a cursor bounces round the enemy area. It starts at the back top corner and runs down the column, then along the row toward MegaMan, bouncing at the edges, one panel every 3 frames.
  - Press the chip button (or wait 6 seconds) and the next 4 panels it leaves are each hit for 150, 28 frames later.
