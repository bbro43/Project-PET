# 0.9.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 10)

New CPU Navi: the real ChargeMan (BN6) — ChrgeMan, EX, SP, RV, BX (1000 / 1500 / 2000 HP; the ROM shortens his name to 8 letters). His AI is decoded from table entry 0x80F6454.
- He never wanders: he attacks in a fixed rhythm, alternating dash and train for five attacks, then the big charge, with a 60-frame pause after each. Afterwards he always returns to the panel he started on.
- Dash: five runs along MegaMan's row, entering from the right edge (50 / 80 / 160).
- Train: five rounds of ChargeMan plus two coal cars across all three rows. Each round uses one of the 8 layouts from the ROM, so there is always a gap to find. ChargeMan does 80 / 130 / 260, the cars 30 / 50 / 100.
- Big charge: pulls three cars down MegaMan's row, whistles at the far edge, backs up, then rushes back at 8–11 px per frame (100 / 150 / 300).
- He can't be hit while he's off the field. Train car sprites come from the game.

Not decoded yet: an inner five-step loop in the big charge, the damage of the cars he pulls in it (his own damage is used), and his flinch rule (only hits of 40+ make him flinch; not simulated).

Asset pack 10: registry adds the ChargeMan and train car sprites, plus ChargeMan's AI parameters.
Tests: 54 engine/Navi tests + 3 local ROM tests.
