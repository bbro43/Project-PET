# 0.17.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 18)

New CPU Navi: CircusMan (BN6) — CrcusMan, EX, SP, RV, BX (700 / 1200 / 1600 / 2100 HP, no element; the ROM spells his name "CrcusMan"). His AI is decoded from table entry 0x80FE890.
- No separate moves: every attack starts with a hop to a new panel.
- Clap: MegaMan's column lights up, then giant hands clap down on it from above and below (20 / 50 / 100 / 130). 1 / 3 / 3 / 5 claps in a row.
- Lion hoop: a flaming hoop goes up at the front of MegaMan's row, and 3 lions (5 at RV/BX) run through it down the row (20 / 50 / 100 / 130).
- Tent: every 5th attack he lights MegaMan's panel, leaps and drops his big top on it. If it catches MegaMan, MegaMan is trapped for 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 seconds, taking 3 / 5 damage every few frames. **Mash any button or direction to break out sooner.** He tries 1 / 3 / 3 / 3 times. Invincibility protects you from being caught.
- The hands, hoop, lion and tent are sequences of his own sprite (LZ77, 64 sequences).

Engine: added a "trapped" player state (no moving or shooting; button mashing shortens it). The battle-over cleanup releases it.
Estimated: clap timing, lion speed, how often the trap hits, and how much each press shortens it. The clap area is read from its highlight code.

Asset pack 18: registry adds the CircusMan sprite and his AI parameters.
Tests: 93 engine/Navi tests + 3 local ROM tests.
