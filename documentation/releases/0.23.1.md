# 0.23.1 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 27)

Falzar movement and shadow fixed after playtesting.
- **Shadow stays on the ground.** His shadow (sequence 40) was glued to his body. His body hangs well below the sprite origin, so his talons dropped past the shadow. Now the body is drawn hovering just above the ground with a slow bob, and the shadow sits under his talons.
- **Moves are glides.** He used to snap to a new spot. Now he glides there over half a second in his idle pose. In the ROM, move 9 is a fade out and back in; the glide is a presentation choice and doesn't change any timing that matters.
- Drill Dive now lines up on your row smoothly before diving.

Asset pack 27: Falzar's registry entry gets a separate shadow sequence.
Tests: 118 engine/Navi tests + 3 local ROM tests.
