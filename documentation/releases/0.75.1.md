# 0.75.1 — Chip lockout from the game

## Fixed
- **Chip lockout:** after a chip's action ends, the game blocks the next chip for that chip's own lockout. The number comes from each chip's data, and moving and the buster stay free. The app now does the same.
  - 10 frames: the seeds and Lance.
  - 20 frames: FireHit, Boomer, GolmHit, JustcOne, FullCust, BusterUp, the plus chips, the arms, SyncTrgr, FlmHook, CornFsta, ParaShl, DestPuls and StreamHd.
  - 30 frames: TimeBom and the Recov chips.
  - 50 frames: AirHocky.
  - Every other chip has none.
- **BusterUp, Atk+10 and WhiCapsl** had used that 20 as their action length. They now finish their action straight away and the 20-frame lockout follows.
