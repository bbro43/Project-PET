# 0.33.0 — the last six BN6 viruses, cleaned up

Re-read the AI code for FighterPlane, DarkMech, SnakeArm, Armadillo, Cragger and Nightmare. Several of them did the wrong thing in 0.31.0; they now follow the ROM (assets 36).

- **FighterPlane**: flies forward across the whole field, *through* MegaMan's area (contact damage), off the edge and back round to its panel, twice; the stronger versions come back round in MegaMan's row. Bombing run: each bomb flashes its panel, then bursts with the ROM blast sprite (0x10/0x26).
- **DarkMech**: drawn with its separate sword object (body seq n + sword seq n+6). Every 3rd turn it throws an **elec ball** (ROM sprite 0x10/0x16) that runs along the row to MegaMan's column, then turns up or down it, and **paralyzes** him. When MegaMan is paralyzed, one DarkMech warps beside him (in his area, from behind if needed) and slashes, then warps home. Paralysis is new in the engine: no flinch or flashing, mash to shorten it; length (120 frames) is unverified.
- **SnakeArm**: it is the claw itself. It hops between holes in its area; to attack it pops up two panels in front of MegaMan in his row, the panels flash, and it leaps onto his panel, sits in a hole there, then goes home. Its old hole stays visible while it is out.
- **Armadillo**: rolls 3–5 panels forward (not back and forth), waits, then hops home in a high arc and uncurls. Curl, ball, roll and uncurl use the ROM animations.
- **Cragger**: drawn with its rock-fist layer (sprite 0x10/0x30). It raises the fist while MegaMan's column flashes, then punches it down onto him (the panels above and below too), with a screen shake; the fist stays 63 frames, then returns. Panel cracking is not modelled yet.
- **Nightmare**: rises out of its puddle with the ROM animation, raises its scythe for 16 frames before the column flashes, and moves toward MegaMan's row (front column before a swing; estimated).
- Animation roles for all six now come from the AI code (marked `ai-set` in data/registry.json) instead of visual guesses.

Tests: 162 passing (new: FighterPlane flight + wrap, DarkMech paralysis → slash, SnakeArm pop-up and leap, Armadillo roll and hop home, Cragger column punch). Browser check on all six: no errors.
