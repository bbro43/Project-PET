# 0.27.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 31)

Virus batch 2: five new families, decoded from the ROM, all six versions each. They're in Virus Battle and in the Endless Survival rotation.

- **Piranha** (AI 2): swims to your row. A sonar mark then slides down the row; when it reaches you, Piranha holds (60 → 30 frames by version) and fires a torpedo at 7 px per frame. Damage 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 (Rare 60 / 100).
- **Heady** (AI 3): moves 4 times (12 → 6 frames after each). Then the panel ahead flashes for 20 frames and it launches its head straight down its row at 6.7 px per frame, for 75 frames. Damage 40 / 70 / 90 / 110 (Rare 70 / 120).
- **Swordy** (AI 4), three styles by version:
  - Swordy and SwordySP slash when you're right in front (wide) or two ahead (long); otherwise they step toward your row.
  - Swordy2 and Rare slide along their row toward you and slash.
  - Swordy3 and Rare2 wander, and every 4th turn they warp in front of you to slash.
  - Wind-up 60 / 20 / 40 / 40 / 12 / 20 frames. Damage 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 (Rare 80 / 150).
- **KillerEye** (AI 5): every 120 frames its eye scans a line from the panel ahead, straight or diagonal, cycling by row. If you're caught, after 18 → 6 frames it fires a beam down that line for 120 frames. Damage 50 / 100 / 150 / 200 (Rare 120 / 250).
- **Quaker** (AI 6): waits 30–100 frames at first, then keeps hopping to a panel in your row (60 → 36 frames in the air; it can't be hit while airborne) and slamming down a shockwave (24 / 16 / 11 / 6 frames per panel). Damage 20 / 30 / 100 / 120 (Rare 50 / 120).
