- Virus Battle (Mettaur formations) and Endless Survival.
- In-app "Update ready" banner; 0.1.x installs switch over automatically once.
- Signal Yard prototype kept at /prototype/.
- Asset pack 2: new shipped data files (data/registry.json, data/rules.mjs).
