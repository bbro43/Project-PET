# 0.60.0 — Chip animations, batch 3: burners and wave arms

## Burners (FireBrn1–3, WideBrn1–3)
- A flame on the panel in front spreads one panel ahead every 4 frames, 3 panels in all. WideBrn also spreads up and down after the first panel.
- Flames last 30 frames (FireBrn) or 60 (WideBrn), and each panel cracks when its flame ends.
- The flame is the game's sprite.

## Wave arms (WaveArm1–3, PwrWave1–3)
- A shockwave runs down the row from the panel in front, 6 frames a panel. PwrWave sends three, one in each row.
- It's the game's shockwave, like Mettaur's.
