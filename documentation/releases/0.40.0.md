# 0.40.0 — MegaBuster and chip arms from the ROM

MegaMan's chip actions are now traced from the ROM. The chip family indexes AIAttackJumptable (0x80F05F0) at family − 0x10. Each action sets MegaMan's animation and spawns a t1 0x5 attachment. The attachment's sprite comes from table 0x80B8BD4 and its offset from the arm-position table 0x80188C0. All of these are decoded on device.

| Action | MegaMan animation | Held sprite | Offset (forward, up) |
|---|---|---|---|
| MegaBuster (0x80EB450) | 0xE | buster arm 0x0C/0x03; muzzle flash 0x0C/0x06 on the shot frame | 0,0 / 40,26 |
| Cannon / HiCannon / M-Cannon (0x80EBC28) | 8 | 0x0C/0x01, palette 0/1/2 | 16,24 |
| Vulcan (0x80EBF30) | 0xA | 0x0C/0x1D | 23,25 |
| AirShot (0x80EC8A0) | 9 | 0x0C/0x18 | 18,24 |
| MiniBomb (0x80EB644) | 6 | ball 0x0C/0x02 in hand (animation 0); thrown on frame 9 as t3 0x8 (animation 1, palette 0) | 0,0 |
| Sword (0x80EB862) / CrakShot (0x80EC97C) | 5 / 0xC | (Sword's blade is the existing slash effect) | |

- **Buster fixed:** MegaMan now holds the buster arm, with the muzzle flash, instead of an empty hand pose. The wrong animation (9) is replaced by the game's 0xE.
- **Correction to 0.39.0:** MiniBomb is the ball thrown by t3 0x8, not the dark bomb of t3 0x4A. The ball now leaves his hand on frame 9, as in the game. Its shadow is app-drawn.
- MegaMan's animation numbers for buster, cannon, throw, slash, AirShot, Vulcan and CrakShot are now rom-verified. They were visual guesses before.

Not yet: Vulcan's second firing animation (0xD), the Recov sparkle and hit effects (their effect numbers aren't traced yet), and Sword's blade attachment.

Tests: 180 passing. Browser check with the ROM (buster, Cannon, Vulcan1, AirShot, MiniBomb): no errors.
