New CPU Navi: TomahawkMan (BN6) — TmhkMan, EX, SP, RV, BX (1000 / 1500 / 2000 HP, Wood element; the ROM spells his name "TmhkMan"). His AI is decoded from table entry 0x80F83F4.
- Moves 4 times between attacks, only 2 once he's below half HP.
- Reads your position: if MegaMan stands at the back, he marks MegaMan's column for a second, then throws his tomahawk onto it (40 / 80 / 120). If MegaMan is closer, he steps to the front and swings, after lighting up a 2×3 area (40 / 80 / 120).
- After 6 attacks (3 for EX and up) he raises the axe and chops: a slash wave runs down his row one panel every 5 frames (80 / 160 / 240).
- Game sprites for the thrown axe and the slash wave.
