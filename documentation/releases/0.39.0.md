# 0.39.0 — AreaGrab, shockwave fix, bomb sprite

**Fix: shockwaves stop at holes.** The game's shockwave (type3 0x16, 0x80C6B64 / 0x80C6C14) checks the panel under it every frame and disappears when the panel lacks flag 0x10. In the panel flags table (0x3007924), broken panels (0x14000) lack it and cracked panels (0x10050) have it. So a Mettaur's shockwave now dies at a broken panel, and still rolls over a cracked one, as in the game.

**AreaGrab and PanlGrab** (chip action t4 0x3, 0x80E0754):
- Time stops: the field dims, the chip name shows and the custom gauge waits.
- A light beam (type3 0xF, sprite 0x0C/0x13 decoded on device) drops onto each panel of the first column ahead that isn't fully MegaMan's (0x800D58C). PanlGrab drops one beam on the edge panel of MegaMan's row.
- Beams fall for 32 frames, then hit whoever stands there for 10. A panel is taken only if nobody stands on it. Time resumes 60 frames later.
- Taken panels switch to MegaMan's colours. He can walk on them and enemies can't.
- After 1800 frames (object_setPanelAllianceTimerLong, 0x708) a stolen panel returns to the enemy once nobody stands on it. The game's exact hand-back order (0x800C746) isn't fully decoded.
- Estimated: the dim and chip-name lengths.

**MiniBomb** now uses the game's bomb sprite (type3 0x4A, sprite 0x0C/0x24). Its shadow stays on the floor while the bomb arcs. That the MiniBomb chip spawns this object is recalled, not traced.

Tests: 180 passing (new: shockwave vs broken and cracked panels; AreaGrab freeze, steal, beam damage and return). Browser check with the ROM: no errors.
