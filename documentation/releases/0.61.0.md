# 0.61.0 — Chip animations, batch 4: shells and ElecPulses

## Shells (IronShl1–3, ParaShl)
- The game's rolling shell (the Armadillo ball, in each chip's colour) rolls to the far edge, hitting everything in its row. It moves 6, 7 or 8 pixels a frame by level and stops at a hole.
- ParaShl rolls three shells, one in each row.

## ElecPulses (ElcPuls1–3, DestPuls)
- The pulse is held out for 60 frames. It hits the panel in front and the whole column after it, once per enemy.
- **ElcPuls1:** paralyses.
- **ElcPuls2:** pulls the enemy one panel closer.
- **DestPuls:** the bug it puts on enemies isn't in the app yet.
- The pulse is the game's sprite, in its colour for each chip.

## Fixed
- StarFish no longer bubbles MegaMan. The ROM value I'd read as "bubble" (in 0.56.0) is the ordinary hit most attacks use.
