# 0.26.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 30)

Virus batch 1: Mettaur, Champy and Gunner are now decoded from the ROM, replacing behaviour I'd written from memory. The virus AI table is at 0x8109050, indexed by the AI byte × 4.

**Mettaur** (0x8109EF4):
- One Mettaur acts at a time. On its first turn it waits 30 frames. It steps one row toward you per turn (3-frame vanish and appear, then 30 / 24 / 18 / 12 frames), and once level with you it swings.
- The swing lasts 64 frames, and the shockwave starts on frame 37. The turn passes on 40 frames after the swing ends.
- Shockwave speed is 22 / 16 / 11 / 6 frames per panel by version. Damage is 10 / 30 / 50 / 70 (Rare 50 / 100).
- While waiting their turn, every version above the base hides under its helmet and can't be hurt.

**Champy** (0x810CD60):
- Acts only when you're in its row and the panel in front of you is free. It vanishes, reappears in front of you after 13 frames, pauses (21 / 18 / 12 / 6 frames), punches (the hit lands on frame 3), holds, and goes home.
- ChampySP and the Rare versions follow with a second punch from your new front panel.
- Damage is 20 / 30 / 50 / 80 (Rare 30 / 90). That's the punch's own table; the old value was the stat record's damage field.

**Gunner** (0x8113078):
- Even versions (Gunner, Sniper, Rare) shoot only when you're in their row. The crosshair slides from Gunner down the row at 3 / 4 / 4.3 px per frame. When it reaches you it locks, and after 24 / 12 frames Gunner fires 3 shots, 10 frames apart, at that panel.
- Odd versions (Shooter, GunnerSP, Rare2) start the crosshair on the front panel of your area and sweep it one panel per shot, bouncing at the edges: 5 / 7 / 7 shots.
- Damage per shot is 10 / 30 / 50 / 70 (Rare 40 / 90).

Mettaurs are the only viruses that take turns; Champy and Gunner act on their own.
