# 0.74.0 — Batch 14: AirRaid and Sensor

## New chips
- **AirRaid1–3:** a fighter plane appears on the panel ahead and rises for half a second. It then drops a bomb every 10 frames: 10 bombs for AirRaid1, 14 for AirRaid2, 18 for AirRaid3.
  - Each bomb goes on the next enemy's panel in turn. Now and then (2 in 8) it goes on a random enemy panel instead, and on the back column of the plane's row when no enemy is left.
  - Each target panel flashes for 6 frames before the blast.
  - The plane has 30, 40 or 50 HP and leaves after 2.5, 3.2 or 3.8 seconds.
- **Sensor1–3:** a sensor eye on the panel ahead, aimed by its row.
  - In the top row it aims diagonally down, in the middle row straight ahead, and in the bottom row diagonally up.
  - After about a second it starts scanning: a dot steps one panel along the line every 8 frames and starts over at the edge.
  - When the dot lands on an enemy, a laser covers the whole line for 100 frames and hits each enemy on it once. The sensor leaves 2 seconds later.
  - The sensor has 20, 30 or 40 HP and otherwise stays 12 seconds.

## Notes
- Which of the laser's three animations goes with which aim is a visual reading.
