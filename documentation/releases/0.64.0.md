# 0.64.0 — Chip animations, batch 6: Spreader, WideShot, TankCannon, MachGun, AquaNeedle, Tornado

- **Spreadr1–3:** a shot that splashes the 3×3 area around the enemy it hits.
- **WideSht / SuprSpr:** the game's water wave sweeps forward across three rows. SuprSpr moves twice as fast.
- **TankCan1–3:** hits the first enemy in the row. If the row is empty, it blasts the back column's three panels and cracks them. It still shows the Cannon arm; its own arm isn't decoded yet.
- **MachGun1–3:** 9 shots into the nearest enemy column, sweeping up and down it. When the nearest column changes, the aim follows it.
- **AquaNdl1–3:** 3 needles, one onto each enemy. With two enemies the weaker one takes two needles; a lone enemy takes all three.
- **Tornado:** a twister 2 panels ahead hits 8 times. On a volcano, grass or ice panel it takes that panel's element, does double damage and turns the panel normal.
- **Static:** its twisters multiply with MegaMan's bugs: 1, 2, 4 or 6 of them.
