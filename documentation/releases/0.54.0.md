# 0.54.0 — Navi Customizer: the game's descriptions, Cross parts in game colours, every part working

## INFORMATION box
- Shows the game's own descriptions ("Won't be pushed!", "B+Left= Strong shield!", "CustmScr Slected Chip=10" …) from the LZ77 text archive CompText873ECC8 (Gregar 0x73CC04). Entry n is program n, up to 3 lines of 8 characters, and the button glyphs are written as A / B.
- The text now stays inside the box. The battle font gained the apostrophe (charmap 0xA9).

## Cross parts
- Each Cross part now uses one of the game's six colours, so it counts towards the colour limit and the same-colour rule like any program:
  - Gregar Crosses: Heat red, Elec yellow, Slash white, Erase pink, Charge green.
  - Falzar Crosses: Spout blue, Tomahawk green, Tengu red, Ground yellow, Dust white.

## Every part, in battle (traced in the ROM; see the audit)
- **SuperArmor:** no flinch. ("Won't be pushed" has nothing to act on yet: nothing in the app pushes MegaMan.)
- **FstBarr:** the battle starts with a barrier that soaks the first 10 damage (loc_80138D2 → sub_80E0D98). The 10 is the Barrier chip's value and is unverified.
- **Shield / Reflect / AntiDmg (B+Left):** hold B and press Left (power attacks 0x3B / 0x8B / 0x3D).
  - Shield and Reflect guard for 20 frames (family 0x2B, 0x14). Reflect answers a blocked hit with a 50-damage shot down the row (sub_80C4FFE).
  - AntiDmg's window is 30 frames (family 0x5A). A hit inside it does no damage, and a 50-damage star goes at the attacker.
  - The wait before the next use (40 / 70, the Unk_05 values) is unverified.
  - The visuals are app-drawn for now.
- **FlotShoe:** no panel effects. Stepping off a cracked panel doesn't break it; poison, ice and grass/ice/holy hit effects don't apply.
- **AirShoes:** can step onto holes.
- **UnderShirt:** as before.
- **ChpShufl:** press L in the custom screen to deal the unpicked chips again, once per battle (sub_802A5DA).
- **NumbrOpn:** the custom screen deals 10 chips (sub_802A40C → sub_802A5A0).
- **AutoHeal:** (max HP + 9) / 10 back after each win or Survival wave (sub_8014040).
- **Encounter programs** stand in for the game's random encounters in Survival (the app's reading):
  - OilBody / Fish / Battery / Jungle: half the picks are viruses of that element.
  - SneakRun: no base-version viruses.
- **Rush / Beat / Tango:** the game's own text says "VS only!" (NetBattles), so they stay off until multiplayer.
- **Collect, Millions, Humor, Poem, SlipRunr:** overworld programs (chip drops, Zennys, the L button, poems, running). Nothing to do in battle.
- **Bugs:** still counted and shown on RUN but not applied in battle.
