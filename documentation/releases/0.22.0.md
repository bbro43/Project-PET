# 0.22.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 23)

New CPU boss: Cybeast Gregar (BN6), AI entry 20 (0x8103C74). HP comes from his spawn hook, which overrides the stats table: 2500 / 3000 / 4000 / 5000.
- He fills the whole enemy area (the ROM reserves all 9 panels), and he's hit on any of them, so the buster always connects.
- His pattern repeats:
  - Two leaps. Each landing shakes the screen and sends a shock down MegaMan's row (50 / 150).
  - Five claw rounds. Each is a Claw Swipe (front column, 3 rows) or a Claw Lunge (two front panels of MegaMan's row), chosen at random (100 / 300). Every 4th round is Beast Thunder instead: lightning on MegaMan's panel three times (70 / 210).
  - Then a big attack. Above half HP it's Beast Breath: three lit targets, the first on MegaMan (60 / 180). Below half HP, Breath and Giga Rush take turns; Giga Rush hits MegaMan's whole row (180 / 540).

Decoded: the decision counters, the HP override, the panel reservation, every damage table and the timing tables.
Estimated: the attack shapes and the landing shock, how each timing table maps to warning or wait, and the hurtbox (every panel).

Sprite: only the Gregar ROM has his full battle sprite; the Falzar ROM's copy is a small 31-sequence block. If your sprites come from Falzar, the app now borrows the sprite from the Gregar ROM when it's also imported; with only Falzar, he shows as placeholder art.
His sprite is five stacked layers (shadow, body, head, forelegs, mane). The app now composites layered sprites, and the layer order is a visual guess.

Asset pack 23: the registry adds the Gregar sprite (with layers), and the rules add his AI parameters.
Tests: 114 engine/Navi tests + 3 local ROM tests.
