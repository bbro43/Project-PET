# 0.69.0 — Three fixes from the game's data, and batch 10: barriers, Invisibl, panel chips, cubes

## Fixed from the ROM (these were from memory before)
- **BlkBomb / FlshBom blast:** their hit area 0x82 is every panel of one side, the panels the game marks as the enemy's (byte_8019C34). So it hits every enemy standing on an enemy panel. A bomb set off on MegaMan's side would hit MegaMan instead.
- **LilBolr:** the boiler starts with 4000 HP minus the chip's damage, and its HP can't drop below 3001. Its burst deals the HP it has lost, so the chip's damage plus what it absorbed, at most 999.
- **Dragon body pieces:** the game gives them no hit area, so only the head hits. This matches what the app already did.
- **FstBarr's barrier:** confirmed as the 10 HP Barrier. The program sets barrier type 1.

## New chips
- **Barrier / Barr100 / Barr200:** a barrier that soaks 10, 100 or 200 damage.
- **BblWrap:** blocks one hit and grows back 4 seconds after it pops. An Elec hit pops it for good.
- **LifeAur:** hits under 200 do nothing; a bigger hit breaks it. It lasts 50 seconds.
- **Invisibl:** MegaMan flashes and attacks pass through him for 6 seconds.
- **PnlRetrn:** MegaMan's side becomes normal panels again (holes included).
- **HolyPanl:** turns the panel under MegaMan holy.
- **Snctuary:** turns all of MegaMan's side holy.
  These three panel chips change the panels after a 64-frame wait, as the game does.
- **RockCube / IceCube:** a 200 HP cube on the panel ahead. It blocks shots and lasts 100 seconds.
