# 0.19.0 — 27 Nov 2026 (asset pack 20)

New CPU Navi: ElementMan (BN6) — ElmntMan, EX, SP, RV, BX (900 / 1300 / 1700 / 2000 HP; the ROM spells his name "ElmntMan"). His decision logic, timings and damage come from table entry 0x81002A8. **This completes the 16 BN6 Navis in the game's Navi AI table.**
- Moves 3 times between attacks (2 above the base version).
- Tower: a tower bursts up under MegaMan's panel (60 / 100 / 140 / 240). It takes turns with his element attack, and sometimes skips the moves before his next attack.
- Every 3 attacks he changes element. His weakness changes with it.
  - Fire: five bursts on MegaMan's panel (30 / 50 / 70 / 120).
  - Aqua: icicles one after another on MegaMan's panel, 4–6 of them (6–10 below half HP) (60 / 90 / 120 / 200).
  - Elec: lightning runs down his row (60 / 100 / 140 / 240).
  - Wood: spikes burst up on the 3 panels ahead of him (30 / 50 / 70 / 120).

Partial: the elemental attacks use plain coloured effects, because their own sprites (objects 0x8D–0x90 and 0x57) aren't mapped yet. How he picks his next element isn't decoded (random, never the current one). His colours don't change with his element yet. The shapes of the elec and wood attacks are estimated.

Asset pack 20: registry adds the ElementMan sprite and his AI parameters.
Tests: 101 engine/Navi tests + 3 local ROM tests.
